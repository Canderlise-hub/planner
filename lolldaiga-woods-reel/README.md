# Lolldaiga Woods — 30" Reel

Two deliverables for the "Escape the Ordinary" campaign film:

- **`director-treatment.md`** — the production-ready, frame-accurate shot list: every cut, transition name, on-screen line and VO cue against a timecode, plus the sound-design and shoot-list notes an editor or DP would need to actually make this.
- **`animatic.html`** — a self-contained, playable preview of the treatment above. It runs the real 30-second timing, typography and transition language (hard cuts, the type-as-mask reveal, freeze-frame, split-screen merge, the smash-cut, the black frame before the logo) so the pacing can be judged before a shoot.

The animatic uses abstract graded color plates in place of footage — no photography of the property exists yet, so nothing here claims to depict the real place. Each plate is labelled with what it stands in for (e.g. "Aerial · wide"), matching the shot list. Swap the `.plate-*` backgrounds for real graded frames once footage exists and the timing/typography carries over unchanged.

Open `animatic.html` in a browser and tap the film to play.

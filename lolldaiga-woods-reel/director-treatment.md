# LOLLDAIGA WOODS — "ESCAPE THE ORDINARY"
### Director's treatment · 30" vertical film · IG Reels / TikTok / Shorts master

Format 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24fps or 30fps · deliver H.264 + a captioned burn-in master for sound-off autoplay.
No voiceover talent is cast yet — VO lines below are written to be recorded dry, close-mic, unhurried, almost a whisper at the top and bottom, slightly warmer/faster mid-film.

This is the frame-accurate shot list behind `animatic.html` in this folder, which plays the piece back as a timed CSS animatic (graded-plate stand-ins, not final photography) so pacing and typography can be judged before a shoot or edit.

---

## Visual system

**Palette** — charcoal `#1C1A15`, ivory `#F2EDE1`, dry-grass gold `#C7A868`, moss `#454B36`, muted bark `#6B4A34`, ember `#B9633B` (reserved for the sunset beat only — do not let it become a second accent).

**Type** — Archivo (Black/900 for graphic typography, tight negative tracking at large sizes) is the voice of the film; Fraunces italic is used exactly twice, as a quiet counter-accent, never for a full headline; JetBrains Mono carries timecodes, frame tags and the caption track — it should read like a slate, not a subtitle app.

**Cut logic** — every transition in the film is named below so the editor never defaults to a crossfade. No two consecutive transitions repeat. Hard cuts land on the beat; mask/reveal and slide moments are the only motion that's allowed to take longer than 12 frames.

**Sound design note** — cut hard sound-effects (a single low woodblock / a soft air-cut whoosh) under BLACK-01, the mask reveal, the split-screen merge, and the smash-cut in Scene 4. Music should drop out entirely under the black frame in Scene 6 — silence is the effect.

## Emotional structure (unchanged from brief, confirmed against the cut above)

0–4 curiosity → 4–9 discovery → 9–14 contrast → 14–19 intimacy → 19–25 connection → 25–30 calm/desire.

Governing rule for the edit: **the story is always predictable, the transition language never is.** If two adjacent transitions in the timeline above ever repeat, that's a note to fix before the film ships — it's the one thing this treatment is built to prevent.
